# Aus Rice (Pre-monsoon Rice) — Cultivation Package & Practices

Source: Bangladesh Rice Research Institute (BRRI) and Department of Agricultural Extension (DAE) / BAMIS Aus rice package of practices
http://www.brri.gov.bd/
https://www.bamis.gov.bd/en/croppnp/1/all/all/

Crop: Aus rice, the pre-monsoon (Kharif-1) rice crop of Bangladesh — the third rice season alongside Boro (dry season) and Aman (monsoon).

## Varieties
- **BRRI dhan48, BRRI dhan82, BRRI dhan98**: modern high-yielding Aus varieties, short duration (~100-110 days), suited to direct seeding.
- Aus is grown either direct-seeded (broadcast/line) on early rains or transplanted where early irrigation is available.

## Season & Sowing Window
- Season: Kharif-1 (pre-monsoon / early summer).
- Sowing window: mid-March to end of April; harvested July-August before or into the monsoon.
- The key timing risk is at the *start*: Aus is sown into the hot, dry pre-monsoon, so early growth usually needs irrigation until the monsoon rains arrive.

## Seed Rate & Sowing
- Direct-seeded: about 20 kg/acre (broadcast) of clean, healthy seed.
- Transplanted Aus uses less seed (~12-15 kg/acre) raised in a seedbed.

## Fertilizer Doses and Timing (kg/bigha; multiply by 3 for kg/acre)
- Aus needs less nitrogen than Boro because of its shorter duration and lower yield potential.
- Approximate basal dose: TSP 10, MoP 9, Gypsum 7 kg/bigha, applied at final land preparation.
- Urea is applied in two top-dressings of about 11 kg/bigha each: the first at early tillering (~15 days) and the second at maximum tillering (~35 days). Do not apply all the urea at once.

## Irrigation
- Establishment (0-25 days): irrigate through the dry pre-monsoon spell until the rains set in.
- Reproductive stage (55-80 days): keep the field saturated at flowering, the most drought-sensitive stage; a dry spell here sharply cuts grain set.

## Pest Risk
- **Yellow stem borer** causes dead hearts in the vegetative stage; treat with Cartap or Fipronil at first dead-hearts.
- **Rice hispa** scrapes parallel white streaks along the leaf blade in the early crop; hand-net the beetles and spray Chlorpyrifos only if damage is severe.

## Harvest & Post-Harvest
- Matures in about 105 days; harvest when 80% of the grains in the panicle turn straw-coloured.
- Timely harvest matters because late Aus overlaps the monsoon and wet grain is hard to dry.

## Soil Suitability
- Best on medium clay-loam soils that hold water, but Aus tolerates lighter loam and sandy-loam better than Boro.
- Heavy clay is workable; very sandy soils drain too fast for the dry early stage unless irrigation is reliable.
