# Lentil (Masur) — Cultivation Notes, BARI Varieties

Source: Journal of Plant Registrations, "'BARI Masur-8': A high-yielding and biofortified lentil cultivar in Bangladesh" and "'BARI Masur-9': An extra-early lentil cultivar for a rice-based cropping system in Bangladesh" (Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute varieties)
https://acsess.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/plr2.20210
https://acsess.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/plr2.20212

## Varieties
- **BARI Masur-8**: matures in 115-120 days, high yield potential ~1,973 kg/ha, fits well into existing rice-based cropping patterns, has late-planting tolerance (can be sown up to first week of December after harvesting long-duration rice).
- **BARI Masur-9**: extra-early variety, matures in under 93 days, average seed yield ~1,201 kg/ha, fits a monsoon rice - lentil - spring rice triple cropping pattern.

## Sowing Time
- Optimum sowing: around 20 November.
- Late sowing: up to 10 December (BARI Masur-8 tolerates later sowing better than most cultivars).
- Season: Rabi (dry season), typically follows a monsoon rice harvest.

## Seed Rate
~12 kg/ha.

## Fertilizer Dose (basal)
- Urea (N): 40 kg N/ha
- TSP (P₂O₅): 60 kg/ha
- MoP (K₂O): 40 kg/ha

## Water Need
Low — lentil is grown as a residual-moisture Rabi crop after rice, typically with minimal or no irrigation, making it a strong option where water availability is limited.
