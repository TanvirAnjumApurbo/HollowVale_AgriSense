# Jute — Cultivation Notes

Source: Bangladesh Jute Research Institute-affiliated agronomic research and agro-economic studies on jute cultivation in Bangladesh
(compiled from published fertilizer-trial and agro-economic research on Tossa/jute cultivation in Bangladesh)

## Fertilizer Dose
Recommended rate: N100 P5 K30 S10 (kg/ha), applied as Urea, TSP, MoP, and Gypsum respectively.

## Fertilizer Application Timing
Two documented split-timing approaches appear in the research:
- Half the urea dose applied as top dressing, remaining fertilizers (TSP, MoP, Gypsum) applied basal at final land preparation, with the rest of the urea applied ~45 days after sowing (DAS).
- Alternative: half of Urea + TSP + MoP + MgSO4 applied at final land preparation, remaining half of urea applied at 42-50 DAS when plant height reaches 10-12 cm.

## Sowing Time
Average sowing window: 1 April to 3 May (Kharif-1 season).

## Seed Rate
~9.26 kg/ha average.

## Water Need
Medium — jute is typically rainfed during the early monsoon (Kharif) season and does not require standing water like Boro rice, but benefits from consistent moisture during establishment.
