# Boro Rice (Paddy) — Cultivation Package & Practices

Source: Bangladesh Agro-Meteorological Information Service (BAMIS), Department of Agricultural Extension
https://www.bamis.gov.bd/en/croppnp/1/all/75/

Crop: Boro Paddy (winter/dry-season rice)

## Sowing / Transplanting
- Seedlings are transplanted at 35-45 days of age.
- 1-2 cm standing water is required at transplanting.
- Recommended arrangement: 2-3 seedlings per hill, planted at 2-3 cm depth.
- Row spacing: 20-25 cm. Plant (hill) spacing: 15-20 cm.

## Fertilizer Doses (kg per Bigha; 1 Bigha ≈ 0.33 acre / 0.1338 ha)
| Land type | Urea | TSP | MP | Gypsum | Zinc Sulphate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lowland Boro (>150 days duration) | 40 | 13 | 22 | 15 | 1.5 |
| Medium land Boro (<150 days duration) | 35 | 12 | 20 | 15 | 1.5 |
| Haor region | 27 | 12 | 22 | 8 | 1.5 |

## Fertilizer Application Timing
- Urea is split into three doses: at land preparation, at tiller emergence (20-25 days after transplanting), and 5-7 days before panicle initiation.
- TSP, MP, Gypsum, and Zinc Sulphate are applied fully at land preparation (basal dose).

## Irrigation Schedule
- 2-3 cm standing water for the first 12-15 days after transplanting.
- Alternate wetting and drying with 5-7 cm water maintained until flowering.
- 2-5 cm water maintained during grain hardening (late grain-fill) stage.

## Weeding
- Hand weeding or weedicides applied before fertilizer top-dressing.

## Water Need
High — this is a flood-irrigated crop; not suitable where water availability is limited unless supplemental irrigation is reliable.

## Harvest & Post-Harvest
Boro matures about 145-160 days after transplanting; harvest at 80-85% golden grain, typically late April to May. Harvest promptly before pre-monsoon (Kalbaishakhi) storms to avoid lodging and grain shattering.

## Soil Suitability
Best on clay and clay-loam soils that hold irrigation water; loam is workable where irrigation is reliable. Sandy and sandy-loam soils drain too fast for a flood-irrigated crop and are a poor fit unless irrigation is very reliable.
