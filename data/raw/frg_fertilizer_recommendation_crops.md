# Fertilizer Recommendation for Crops (FRG-2024) — Doses for AgriSense Crops

Source: Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council (BARC), Fertilizer Recommendation Guide (FRG) 2024, Section 7 "Fertilizer Recommendation for Crops"
http://www.barc.gov.bd/

## How to read these numbers (units matter)
FRG-2024 gives recommended doses as **elemental nutrient in kg per HECTARE** (N, P, K, S, Zn, B, Mg — not fertilizer material). Each crop has a **yield goal** and four soil-test fertility classes — Optimum, Medium, Low, Very low — with a **lower soil-test value needing a higher dose**. Most Bangladesh soils test Low to Medium, so the Medium/Low bands below are the practical defaults.

To turn a nutrient recommendation into sacks of fertilizer a farmer buys: convert nutrient to material (Urea ~46% N, TSP ~20% P, MoP ~50% K, Gypsum ~18% S, Zinc sulphate ~23-33% Zn), then convert per-hectare to per-acre by dividing by 2.471. AgriSense's costed season plan uses per-acre material doses derived exactly this way.

## Cereals

### Boro rice (dry-season irrigated) — yield goal 7.5 t/ha (high-yielding varieties)
Medium-fertility soil: N 59-116, P 7-12, K 41-80, S 7-12, Zn 0.0-1.3 kg/ha. Low-fertility soil raises N to 117-174 kg/ha.
Apply all P, K, S and Zn as basal at final land preparation. Split nitrogen in three equal top-dressings: basal/after seedling establishment, early tillering, and 5-7 days before panicle initiation. About 30% of urea-N is saved using Urea Super Granule / deep placement.

### Aus rice (pre-monsoon) — yield goal 5.0 t/ha
Medium-fertility soil: N 25-48, P 5-8, K 23-44, S 4-6, Zn 0.0-0.7 kg/ha. Aus needs distinctly less nitrogen than Boro because it is a shorter, lower-yield crop.

### Transplanted Aman rice (monsoon) — yield goal 6.0 t/ha
Medium-fertility soil: N 31-60, P 6-10, K 28-54, S 5-8, Zn 0.0-0.9 kg/ha. N for T. Aman can be cut by 25-30 kg/ha if 12-15 t/ha of dhaincha (Sesbania) green manure is incorporated before planting.

### Wheat (irrigated) — yield goal 4.5 t/ha
Medium-fertility soil: N 41-80, P 11-20, K 31-60, S 6-10, Mg 0-4, Zn 0.0-1.3, B 0.0-1.0 kg/ha.
Apply two-thirds of N plus all P, K, S, Mg, Zn, B as basal; top-dress the remaining one-third N after the first irrigation at 17-21 days after sowing.

### Maize — yield goal 11 t/ha (the heaviest feeder here)
Medium-fertility soil: N 81-160, P 19-36, K 46-90, S 13-24, Mg 6-10, Zn 0.0-2.0, B 0.0-1.0 kg/ha. For the Kharif (monsoon) crop the dose is reduced by about 30%. Apply one-third N at sowing in furrows, the rest split at 50-55 days and at tasselling.

## Fibre — Jute
### Tossa jute (Corchorus olitorius) — yield goal 4.4 t/ha
Medium-fertility soil: N 36-70, P 7-12, K 26-50, S 7-12, Zn 0.0-2.0 kg/ha.
Apply half of N plus all P, K, S, Zn as basal; top-dress the remaining N at 40-45 days after sowing. White jute (C. capsularis, 3.5 t/ha goal) is slightly lower: N 31-60, P 5-8, K 23-44, S 7-12.

## Pulses (nitrogen-fixing — Rhizobium replaces most N)
### Lentil / Masur — yield goal 1.8 t/ha
Medium-fertility soil: N 8-14, P 6-10, K 9-16, S 5-8, Zn 0-1.0, B 0-0.4, Mo 0-0.4 kg/ha. Apply all fertilizer as basal. If Rhizobium inoculum is used (50 g/kg seed), no nitrogen fertilizer is needed — this is why lentil's fertilizer cost is so low.

### Chickpea / Chola — yield goal 2.0 t/ha
Medium-fertility soil: N 10-18, P 7-12, K 9-16, S 5-8, Zn 0.0-1.0, B 0.0-0.6, Mo 0-0.4 kg/ha. Same basal application and Rhizobium substitution as lentil.

## Oilseed — Mustard / Sarisha — yield goal 2.0 t/ha
Medium-fertility soil: N 31-60, P 9-16, K 26-50, S 9-16, Mg 0-3, Zn 0-1.3, B 0.0-1.0 kg/ha.
Mustard is highly responsive to SULPHUR — never skip the gypsum. Apply half of N plus all P, K, S, Mg, Zn, B as basal; top-dress remaining N at flower initiation (~25 days after sowing).

## High-value vegetables & tuber (need 3-5 t/ha organic manure plus the doses below)
### Potato — yield goal 30 t/ha
Medium-fertility soil: N 46-90, P 11-20, K 46-90, S 6-10, Mg 0-5, Zn 0-2.0, B 0-0.8 kg/ha, plus 3 t/ha organic fertilizer. Apply all P, S, Mg, Zn, B and half of N and K as basal; side-dress the remaining half of N and K at 30-35 days during earthing-up.

### Onion — yield goal 20 t/ha
Medium-fertility soil: N 31-60, P 11-20, K 26-50, S 11-20, Zn 0-1.3, B 0.0-0.7 kg/ha, plus 5 t/ha organic fertilizer. Onion is a heavy sulphur feeder; potassium is best supplied as K2SO4 rather than MoP. Split N and K at 25 and 50 days after planting.

### Chilli / Morich — yield goal 2.5 t/ha
Medium-fertility soil: N 26-50, P 13-24, K 31-60, S 5-8, Zn 0-1.0, B 0.0-0.7 kg/ha, plus 3 t/ha organic fertilizer. Apply all P, K, S, Zn, B and half N as basal; split the rest of N at 25, 50 and 70 days after planting.

### Tomato (winter) — yield goal 75 t/ha
Medium-fertility soil: N 41-80, P 16-30, K 26-50, S 8-14, Mg 5-8, Zn 0-1.6, B 0-1.0 kg/ha, plus 5 t/ha organic fertilizer. Boron guards against fruit cracking. Split N and K at 15 and 35 days after transplanting.
