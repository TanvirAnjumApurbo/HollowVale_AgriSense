# Rice — Common Pests, Favorable Conditions, and Treatment

Source: Bangladesh Agro-Meteorological Information Service (BAMIS), Department of Agricultural Extension
https://www.bamis.gov.bd/en/pests/1/all/76/

| Pest | Favorable conditions | Growth stage at risk | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rice Case Worm | Relative humidity above 90%, cloudy | Early to maximum tillering | Malathion or Chlorpyrifos |
| Rice Hispa | Temp 32-35°C max / 24-26°C min; humidity 96-99% max / 75-81% min | Seedling to flowering | Chlorpyrifos or Cypermethrin |
| Green Leaf Hopper | — | — | Malathion or Chlorpyrifos |
| Mealy Bug | — | Maximum tillering | Malathion or Chlorpyrifos |
| Rat | — | All stages | Recommended poison bait or traps |
| Long Horn Cricket | — | — | Malathion or Chlorpyrifos |
| White Backed Plant Hopper | — | Maximum tillering | Malathion or Chlorpyrifos |
| Ear Cutting Caterpillar | — | Heading stage | Cypermethrin or Lambdacyhalothrin |
| Gall Midge | Temp above 33°C, afternoon humidity below 71%, sunshine above 7.4 hrs | Seedling to flowering | Granular/liquid formulations |
| Grasshopper | Cooler temps, higher humidity, more rainfall | Early tillering to maturity | Malathion or Chlorpyrifos |
| Yellow Stem Borer | Min temp above 20.3°C (optimal 24-29°C), high morning humidity, dry weather | Early tillering to flowering | Carbofuran, Cartap, Fipronil, or Diazinon |
| Leaf Roller/Folder | Temp above 31.9°C max / below 21.6°C min, high humidity, cloudy | Mid-tillering to dough stage | Malathion or Chlorpyrifos |
| Brown Plant Hopper | Temp above 32°C, humidity 80-90%, light rainfall below 75mm | Maximum tillering to dough stage | Isoprocarb or Imidacloprid |
| Rice Bug | High max temp, low min temp, high morning humidity, low afternoon humidity, minimal rainfall | Flowering to milking stage | Malathion or Chlorpyrifos |

Use this table to flag pest risk when the forecasted weather (temperature, humidity, rainfall) for the farm's location and the rice crop's current growth stage match a pest's favorable conditions.
