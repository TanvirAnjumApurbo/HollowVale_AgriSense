# Crop Calendar of Bangladesh — Sowing, Harvest and Per-Acre Seed Rate

Source: Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council (BARC), Crop Calendar of Bangladesh (§1.8), Fertilizer Recommendation Guide 2024 reference materials
http://www.barc.gov.bd/

This is the national reference for **time of sowing/transplanting, time of harvest, and per-acre seed requirement** of the major crops. Windows are expressed as month ranges; a specific field's date is chosen within the window from the weather and the preceding crop.

## Kharif-1 and Kharif-2 (monsoon rice and jute)

**Aus rice (HYV, transplanted)**: sow/transplant mid-March to mid-April; harvest July to August. Per-acre seed requirement 11-14 kg (transplanted); broadcast HYV needs only 8-9 kg.

**Transplanted Aman rice (HYV)**: transplant late June to mid-August; harvest December to early January. Per-acre seed requirement 8-9 kg.

**Boro rice (HYV / hybrid)**: sow the seedbed December to mid-February and transplant about 40 days later; harvest mid-April to June. Per-acre seed requirement 8-11 kg. (Local Boro is sown mid-November to mid-January.)

**Tossa jute (Corchorus olitorius)**: sow mid-April to early May; harvest August to September. Per-acre seed requirement 4-5 kg. White jute (C. capsularis) is sown earlier, early March to mid-April, harvested July to August.

## Rabi (dry winter season)

**Wheat**: sow November to December; harvest March to mid-April. Per-acre seed requirement is 28-33 kg without irrigation, 33-37 kg with 1-2 irrigations, and 37-47 kg with 4-5 irrigations.

**Maize (Rabi)**: sow mid-October to late December; harvest early April to end May. Per-acre seed requirement 7-9 kg.

**Potato**: plant mid-September to November; harvest mid-January to March. Per-acre seed requirement 4-6 quintals of seed tuber (about 400-600 kg).

**Mustard / rapeseed (Sarisha)**: sow mid-October to mid-November; harvest late January to mid-February. Per-acre seed requirement 3-4 kg. Its short duration lets it fit between Aman and Boro rice.

**Lentil / Masur**: sow mid-October to mid-November; harvest early February to early March. Per-acre seed requirement 11-14 kg.

**Chickpea / Chola**: sow mid-October to late November; harvest early March to early April. Per-acre seed requirement 14-17 kg.

**Onion**: transplant early October to early December; harvest late April to mid-June. Per-acre seed requirement 2-3 kg of true seed, or 2-3 quintals of onion sets (bulbs).

**Chilli / Morich (Rabi)**: sow mid-November to mid-January; the crop is picked 3-4 months after sowing. Roughly 100 g of seed raises the seedlings for one acre. (Kharif chilli is sown mid-April to mid-July.)

**Tomato**: sow mid-August to late November; harvest from early December to mid-January. Per-acre seed requirement is 50-70 g of seed, raising about 6,000-8,000 seedlings.

## How AgriSense uses this
The season plan picks a specific transplanting/sowing date inside the crop's window based on the live weather forecast, then counts forward through the crop's growth stages to place fertilizer, irrigation and harvest dates. The per-acre seed requirement here cross-checks the seed quantity and seed cost in the costed plan.
