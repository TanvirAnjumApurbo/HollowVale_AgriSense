# Maize — Cultivation Package & Practices

Source: Bangladesh Agro-Meteorological Information Service (BAMIS), Department of Agricultural Extension
https://www.bamis.gov.bd/en/croppnp/1/all/8/

## Sowing/Planting Time
- Rabi season: 15 October to 15 November.
- Kharif-1 season: 15 February to 15 March.

## Spacing
- Row-to-row: 60 cm.
- Plant-to-plant along the row: 25 cm or 50 cm depending on variety/density target.

## Fertilizer Doses (kg/ha)
| Fertilizer | Rabi season | Kharif season |
|---|---|---|
| Urea | 172-312 | 216-268 |
| TSP | 168-216 | 132-216 |
| MP | 96-144 | 72-120 |
| Gypsum | 144-168 | 96-144 |
| Zinc Sulphate | 10-15 | 7-12 |
| Boric acid | 5-7 | 5-7 |
| Cow dung (Gobar), tons/ha | 4-6 | 4-6 |

## Fertilizer Application Timing
- One-third of the urea dose is applied as basal at completion of land preparation.
- Remaining urea is split into two top dressings: 25-30 days after germination, and 40-50 days after germination.

## Irrigation Schedule
Four irrigations, at:
1. 15-20 days after sowing
2. 30-35 days after sowing
3. 60-70 days after sowing
4. 85-89 days after sowing

## Weeding
Weeding should be completed within one month of germination.

## Water Need
Medium — needs four scheduled irrigations across the season but does not require standing water like rice.
