# Potato — Fertilizer Management in Bangladesh

Source: "Fertilizer management practices in potato cultivation: a baseline study for the introduction of GE potato in Bangladesh", PMC (National Center for Biotechnology Information)
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11272610/

## Recommended Fertilizer Doses (baseline/official recommendation referenced by the study)
| Nutrient/input | Dose |
|---|---|
| Urea (N) | 250 kg/ha |
| TSP (P) | 150 kg/ha |
| MoP (K) | 250 kg/ha |
| Gypsum (S) | 120 kg/ha |
| Zinc sulfate | 10 kg/ha |
| Magnesium sulfate | 100 kg/ha |
| Boric acid | 10 kg/ha |
| Cow dung | 9,071 kg/ha |

## Application Timing
- Urea and MoP are applied as split doses at the first and second earthing-up.
- TSP (P), Magnesium (Mg), Gypsum (S), and Boron (B) are applied as basal doses during final soil preparation.

## Notes on Real-World Practice
The underlying study (surveying four potato-growing regions of Bangladesh, 2020-2021) found many farmers apply nitrogen well above the recommended dose (up to ~134% of recommendation in the Central/Mid-East region), which is a caution for over-fertilization risk, not a recommendation to exceed the table above.

## Season & Water Need
Potato is a Rabi (dry season, roughly November-April) crop in Bangladesh. Water need is medium — it needs consistent moisture especially during tuber bulking but does not tolerate waterlogging.
