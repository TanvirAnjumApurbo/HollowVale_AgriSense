# Tomato — Cultivation Package & Practices

Source: Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute (BARI) vegetable research and Department of Agricultural Extension (DAE) tomato package of practices
http://www.bari.gov.bd/
https://www.bamis.gov.bd/en/croppnp/1/all/all/

Crop: Tomato, a high-value winter (Rabi) vegetable widely grown across Bangladesh.

## Varieties
- **BARI Tomato-2, -14, -15**: improved winter varieties.
- Hybrid tomatoes are widely grown for high yield; heat-tolerant hybrids allow a summer crop under cover.

## Season & Sowing Window
- Season: Rabi (winter) is the main season; seedlings are transplanted from mid-October to end of November and fruit is harvested December-March.
- Cool, dry winter weather (15-30°C) gives the best fruit set; temperatures above 30°C at flowering cause blossom drop.

## Seed Rate & Sowing
- About 0.15 kg/acre of hybrid seed raised as seedlings in a nursery.
- Transplant 25-30 day old seedlings at roughly 60 cm x 40 cm spacing; staking keeps fruit off the ground and cuts rot.

## Fertilizer Doses and Timing (kg/ha; divide by ~2.47 for kg/acre)
- Urea 220, TSP 180, MoP 150, Gypsum 100 kg/ha, plus Magnesium, Zinc and Boron.
- Boron is important — it guards against fruit cracking and blossom-end rot.
- Timing: apply all TSP, Gypsum, Magnesium, Zinc, Boron and one-third of the urea with half the MoP as a basal dose. Top-dress the remaining urea in two splits (about 25 and 45 days after transplanting) with the rest of the MoP at fruit set.

## Irrigation
- Keep steady, even moisture — irregular watering is the main cause of blossom-end rot and cracked fruit.
- Establishment (5-20 days) and the flowering-to-fruiting window (40-90 days) are the critical periods; avoid waterlogging, which spreads root and wilt diseases.

## Pest Risk
- **Tomato fruit borer (Helicoverpa)** bores holes at the calyx end of the fruit; use pheromone traps and spray Emamectin benzoate at egg-lay.
- **Late blight (Phytophthora)** causes water-soaked leaf and stem lesions that spread fast in cool, foggy weather; spray preventively with Mancozeb and switch to Cymoxanil+Mancozeb once symptoms appear.

## Harvest & Post-Harvest
- Fruit is ready about 70-110 days after transplanting; pick at the breaker/pink stage for distant markets and red-ripe for local sale.
- Tomato is perishable, so harvest in the cool morning and grade before transport.

## Soil Suitability
- Best on well-drained fertile loam and sandy-loam soils high in organic matter.
- Heavy waterlogged clay is a poor fit — it promotes bacterial wilt and root rot; use raised beds and good drainage on heavier soils.
